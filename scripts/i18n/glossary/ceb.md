# Cebuano (ceb) glossary: use these exactly

Register: everyday Bisaya as spoken in Cebu and Davao, warm and simple; "ka/nimo/imo" for the player. Cebuano has no
"po": show respect to elders with "Nay", "Tay", "Lola", "palihug". Common English and Tagalog-origin tech words people
really use stay (AI, app, chatbot, post, online, link, deepfake, XP, peke, scam).
